# AcmeCorp Travel and Expense Policy

*Fictional policy for the AWS Scenarios Labs. Version 1.4, effective 1 April 2026.*

## Submitting expenses
- Submit expenses in the finance portal within **30 days** of the purchase.
- Attach an itemised receipt for every expense over **USD 25**.

## Travel
- Book flights through the company travel portal. Economy class for flights under **6 hours**; premium economy for longer flights.
- Hotels: up to **USD 200 per night** (USD 300 in high-cost cities listed in the portal).

## Meals
- Daily meal allowance while travelling: **USD 75**.
- Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Not reimbursed
- Personal entertainment, fines, and upgrades not covered above.

## Approval
- Expenses up to **USD 1,000** are approved by your manager.
- Above USD 1,000, the department head must also approve.
