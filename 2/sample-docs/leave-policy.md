# AcmeCorp Leave Policy

*Fictional policy for the AWS Scenarios Labs. Version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026.*

## Annual leave
- Full-time employees receive **24 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Leave accrues monthly (2 days per month) and can be used once accrued.
- Up to **5 unused days** can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave
- Employees receive **12 days** of paid sick leave per year.
- A doctor's note is required for absences longer than **3 consecutive days**.

## Parental leave
- The primary caregiver receives **26 weeks** of fully paid parental leave.
- The secondary caregiver receives **6 weeks** of fully paid parental leave.
- Parental leave applies equally to birth, **adoption** and surrogacy.
- Leave must start within 12 months of the child's arrival.
- Employees must have completed **6 months** of service to be eligible.

## Bereavement leave
- **5 days** of paid leave for the death of an immediate family member.
- **2 days** for extended family.

## How to request leave
Submit requests in the HR portal at least **2 weeks** in advance for annual leave. Your manager approves or rejects within 3 working days.
