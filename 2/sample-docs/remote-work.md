# AcmeCorp Remote Work Policy

*Fictional policy for the AWS Scenarios Labs. Version 2.0, effective 1 March 2026.*

## Eligibility
- Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week** after completing their probation period (3 months).
- Customer-facing roles agree their remote days with their team lead.

## Working from another country
- Employees may work from another country for up to **30 days per calendar year**.
- Requests must be approved by HR and Legal at least **4 weeks** before travel, because of tax and visa rules.
- Working from countries under trade sanctions is not permitted.

## Equipment
- AcmeCorp provides a laptop and a one-time home office allowance of **USD 500**.
- Company data may only be accessed from company-managed devices.

## Security
- Always connect through the company VPN.
- Never work on confidential documents in public places where screens can be seen.
- Report a lost or stolen device to IT Security within **1 hour**.
